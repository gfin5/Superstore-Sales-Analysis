# Superstore-Sales-Analysis

Analysis of a dataset that includes the sales history of a superstore. This dataset was found on [kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final) The dataset spans from 2014 to 2017. The goal of this analysis was to find trends in the sales data to give reccomendations to the superstore. I wanted to find the regions, time periods, and products that drive the most sales and profit.
Analysis was done on excel and the pivot tables and cleaned data can be found in the .xlsx file.

# Analysis and Recommendations
- The Western Region is the most profitable region, though most of the sales are concentrated in California. Populus States with large central cities like California, New York, and Texas led the states in sales. While California and New York were very profitable, Texas was one of the least profitable. Washington was another very profitable state while Ohio and Pennsylvania were high in sales but low in profit. 
- Sales and profit increased each year, with quarter 4 being the best in both categories year over year. 
- Technology was the highest sales and profit product category. In the Furniture category, Bookcases and tables were 2 standout negative profit items.

- Possible improvements could be to focus more of the sales in the Western Region and big cities as there is large demand for products there. Perhaps you want to create more stores in these areas. 
- You can commit more resources to advertising and producing technology products as they are profitable across the board and generate lots of sales. 
- You may consider stopping producing bookcases and tables as they create negative profit. 
More extremely, you could also stop producing supplies and fasteners in the Office Supplies Category as they are less profitable and contribute to a very small portion of the company's sales.
